Pollinators in Australia

Exploring the geography, seasonality and diversity of Australia's recorded bees, alongside the managed beekeeping industry.

01

Where are Australia's bees?

Bee records are not distributed evenly across Australia. These maps show where recorded diversity is highest, where individual observations occur, and where major recording hotspots emerge.

Values are standardised by recording volume, not population size. Occurrence records also reflect observation and collection effort.

Key takeaway The ACT shows the highest recorded diversity relative to sampling. Western Australia and Tasmania also show high relative richness.
What the maps reveal Bee records are concentrated around populated coastal regions, particularly in the south-east. These patterns also reflect where recording effort is strongest.
02

How does bee activity change through the year?

Recording activity changes substantially across the calendar, and different bee families show different seasonal patterns.

When are bee families most commonly recorded?

Darker cells indicate more recorded bee occurrences.

How much does recording activity vary?

Boxplots summarise monthly variation; dots show individual months.

Seasonal pattern Recording activity is not constant throughout the year. Several bee families are recorded more often in warmer months.
03

Which bee families dominate the records?

Australia's bee occurrence records are dominated by several families, but their contributions differ between states and territories.

Key takeaway Apidae contributes the largest share of occurrence records. Colletidae also represents a substantial share of observations.

Hover over a flow to compare state, family and records.

04

How does wild diversity compare with managed beekeeping?

Recorded bee diversity and managed hive activity differ. High diversity does not always mean more managed hives.

Why this matters High recorded diversity does not imply larger beekeeping industries. The datasets capture different parts of Australia’s pollinator landscape.

The bigger picture

Australia’s bee records vary across geography and seasons.
Queensland and Western Australia show high recorded diversity.
New South Wales has the most managed hives.
Wild bee records differ from managed beekeeping activity.
