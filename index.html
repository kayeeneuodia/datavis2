What does the data tell us?

Australia's recorded bee diversity is geographically and seasonally uneven. Queensland and Western Australia show high recorded taxonomic diversity, while New South Wales has the largest managed hive population among the comparable DAFF regions. The contrast highlights the difference between wild bee biodiversity records and managed beekeeping activity.
