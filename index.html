Pollinators in Australia

Exploring the geography, seasonality and diversity of Australia's recorded bees, alongside managed beekeeping.

01

Where are Australia's bees?

Bee records are not distributed evenly across Australia. These maps show recorded diversity and observations. They also reveal major recording hotspots.

Values are standardised by recording volume, not population size. Occurrence records also reflect recording effort.

Key takeaway

The ACT shows the highest recorded diversity relative to sampling. Western Australia and Tasmania also show high relative richness.

What the maps reveal

Bee records cluster around populated coastal regions. The south-east shows particularly strong recording activity. Recording effort also influences these patterns.

02

How does bee activity change through the year?

Recording activity changes throughout the calendar. Bee families also show different seasonal patterns.

When are bee families most commonly recorded?

Darker cells indicate more recorded bee occurrences.

How much does recording activity vary?

Boxplots summarise variation. Dots represent individual months.

Each family uses its own vertical scale. This highlights its seasonal pattern.

Seasonal pattern

Recording activity changes throughout the year. Several families peak during warmer months.

03

Which bee families dominate the records?

Several families dominate Australia's bee records. Their contributions also vary across locations.

Key takeaway

Apidae contributes the largest share of records. Colletidae also represents a substantial share.

Hover over a flow to compare state, family and records.

04

How does wild diversity compare with managed beekeeping?

Recorded diversity and managed hive activity differ. High diversity does not always mean more hives.

Why this matters

High recorded diversity does not imply a larger beekeeping industry. The datasets describe different pollinator activity.

The bigger picture

Australia’s bee records vary across geography and seasons.
Queensland has high overall recorded bee diversity.
New South Wales has the most managed hives.
Wild records differ from managed beekeeping activity.
